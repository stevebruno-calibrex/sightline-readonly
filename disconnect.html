Disconnecting Sightline Read Only

Calibrex Developments. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Sightline Read Only is an internal Calibrex application that reads financial reports from Calibrex QuickBooks Online company files. It never writes to QuickBooks.

Disconnect from QuickBooks

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as the company's administrator.
  2. Open Apps, then My Apps.
  3. Find Sightline Read Only and choose Disconnect.

You can also disconnect from the Intuit App Store at appcenter.intuit.com/connect/apps.

What happens next

  • Access stops immediately. No further reports are read from that company.
  • The stored copy of that company's figures is deleted from Calibrex systems.
  • Other connected companies are unaffected.

Reconnecting

An authorised Calibrex user can reconnect a company from the Companies panel inside Sightline. Reconnecting requires a fresh consent from the QuickBooks account owner.
